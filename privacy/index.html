Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Konjac Translate is built to work without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

We collect no data

Konjac Translate does not collect, store or share any personal data on any server. There is no account and no sign-in.

Translation, speech recognition and speech happen on your device

Text translation uses Apple’s on-device translation models. Speech recognition and text-to-speech run on your iPhone. What you say and type is not sent to us or to any server. The internet is used only when you choose to download a language from Apple.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you speak a turn in a conversation. Audio is processed on the device and is never saved.

History and cards

Your history, favorites and custom cards are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it does not use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
