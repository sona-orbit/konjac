Two-way

Both sides speak. Both sides understand.

The screen splits in two and the other person’s half turns upside down. You speak your language, they answer in theirs, and every line appears translated on the right side.

  • Speak or type, whichever is easier
  • Replay any line aloud
  • Swap languages with one tap

Offline

No signal? It still translates.

Download a language once and translation, speech recognition and speech all run on your iPhone. On a plane, underground or abroad without data, it just works.

  • Downloaded languages listed first
  • Nothing you say is sent to a server
  • Recordings are never saved

Phrase cards

Keep the sentences you say again and again

Save any translation as a card, or use the ready-made travel cards. Tap one and it is translated into the language of the person in front of you.

  • Decks for travel or work
  • Show a card full screen
  • 3 custom cards free, unlimited with Pro

History and review

Turn what you translated into flash cards

Your conversations and translations are kept by day. Review them as flash cards to remember the phrases you need.

  • Browse by day, with favorites
  • Flash-card review with shuffle
