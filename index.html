Konjac Translate

Talk face to face, keep your own phrase cards, and translate everything right on your iPhone — even offline.

Coming soon to the App Store

iPhone · iOS 18 or later · Free to download

See it in one minute

A short tour of the app on a real iPhone.

What it does

💬

Talk face to face

The screen splits in two and your partner’s half turns upside down, so you can set the phone between you. Speak or type, and each side reads the translation in their own language.

  • On-device speech recognition
  • Replay any line
  • Speaks translations aloud
✍️

Translate text, then show it

Type or paste a sentence and translate it. Copy it, play it aloud, or show it as a big full-screen card to the person in front of you.

  • Copy
  • Speak
  • Full-screen card
🗂️

Your own phrase cards

Keep the phrases you say most — for travel or for work — as cards in decks you name and reorder. Save any translation or history entry as a card; it’s translated into whatever language you pick next time.

  • Decks you name
  • Save from history
  • Built-in travel cards
Ready-made travel cards

Transport, dining, stay, shopping and directions. Tap a card and it’s translated right there; star the ones you use often.

🕘

History and review

Every conversation and translation is kept on your iPhone, grouped by day, with favorites. Review mode turns your history into flash cards.

  • Grouped by day
  • Favorites
  • Flash-card review (Pro)
📴

On-device and offline

Translation uses Apple’s on-device models. Download a language once and it keeps working without a connection — nothing you say or type is sent to a server.

  • About 20 languages
  • Works offline
  • No account

Free to start

Everything you need to talk and translate is free. Pro is for people who build up their own cards.

Free

  • Face-to-face conversation
  • Text translation and full-screen cards
  • Built-in travel cards and history
  • 3 custom cards

Pro

  • Unlimited custom cards and decks
  • Flash-card review of your history
  • Supports independent development

Weekly, monthly, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The App Store shows the price in your local currency.

Stays on your iPhone

  • Translation, speech recognition and speech all happen on the device
  • No account, no ads, no tracking
  • Recordings are never saved
  • History and cards live only on your iPhone
